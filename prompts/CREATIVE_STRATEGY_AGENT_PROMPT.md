Build on **AirWindow Mumbai**, the confidence-aware outdoor activity scheduler for school sports coordinators and outdoor-event organisers. Ask whether an existing one-, two- or three-hour event should move within its feasible range, given forecasts and the strength of historical evidence.

## Required product

- Preserve the original booking in every comparison. Enumerate complete consecutive hourly alternatives within duration/range/horizon; no zero fill, gap bridging or extrapolation.
- Compare means, maxima, peak hours, individual forecasts, signed difference and other feasible options. Numerical rank is not a confident recommendation.
- Separate ranking, same-origin paired historical error, calibration and classification. Support ROBUST IMPROVEMENT, TENTATIVE IMPROVEMENT, NO CLEAR WINNER and INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. Reserve robust/probability/interval claims for independently validated chronological calibration.
- Match station, duration and lead; preserve dependence with origin-block resampling. Report all eligible cases, origin counts, held-out ranking diagnostics, exclusions and failures.
- Keep retrospective replay separate from live forecast data. Never put future actuals into live logic, hidden targets in public assets or fitting code in the browser.
- Print a one-page brief with comparison, evidence/abstention, station, times, model version and limits.
