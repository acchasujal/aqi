# Prize Strategy and Eligibility

**Competition:** Zindi Google Mumbai Data Challenge: Air Quality Mapping & Forecasting<br>
**Research snapshot:** 6 November 2026<br>
**Status:** Confirm live organiser instructions before submitting.

## 1. Treat this as three separate award tracks

| Track | Published award | Main selection basis | Key date / requirement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Main leaderboard | $2,500 / $1,500 / $1,000 for 1st/2nd/3rd | Final private RMSE, code review and rule compliance | Final ML submission: 10 Nov 2026, 23:59:59 IST; private period described as 11–12 Nov |
| Best undergraduate student | $500 | Specific selection method not sufficiently documented in the material reviewed | Confirm student eligibility and evidence required |
| Most Creative / Best Additional Submission | $500 | Impact, novelty, functionality; bonus for Google tools | 13 Nov 2026, 18:29 GMT = 23:59 IST; form asks for working link and one PDF/document <=10 MB |

The competition page also lists 5,000 Zindi points. See the current official page and organiser announcements before relying on this snapshot.

## 2. Official creative category requirements

The organiser says entrants may build a data visualisation, map, different model, app or other product using challenge data and additional ClimateVerse datasets. The submitted work is reviewed by experts from Google, Zindi and ClimateVerse and assessed on impact, novelty and functionality. Bonus points are stated for using Google tools.

Submission form fields in the supplied form copy:
- Email as used on Zindi.
- Zindi username.
- First and last name.
- Relevant submission link (live website, GitHub or other format).
- One PDF or document, maximum 10 MB.
- Availability to travel to Mumbai for the 19 Nov finalists' event.

The pasted organiser comment says the form was re-enabled after a participant reported it closed. Re-open and test the actual form before submission; do not assume it remains open.

## 3. Finalist eligibility and events

- Participation is open across India.
- Only Maharashtra residents are eligible to qualify as finalists for the in-person closing event; proof of Maharashtra residency is required.
- The creative instructions and form refer to a 19 November judging presentation.
- The main overview separately describes a closing event planned for 20 November 2026.

Do not conflate the two event references. Confirm exact dates, venue, travel expectations and whether remote presentations are possible.

## 4. Questions to send to organisers

1. Can a participant receive more than one award (main leaderboard + undergraduate + creative), or are categories mutually exclusive?
2. What is the exact eligibility rule and evidence required for Best Undergraduate Student?
3. Is the creative finalist presentation on 19 Nov, while the main closing event is on 20 Nov? Are both mandatory for the relevant awards?
4. Does a participant need Maharashtra residency to receive the creative award, or only to qualify for the in-person main finalists' event?
5. Is a publicly deployed static app hosted on Firebase Spark acceptable as a Google-tool use case?
6. Are ClimateVerse catalogue data and APIs permitted for the creative category under their specific provider terms, including the Open-Meteo access tier?
7. Does the creative submission need to be fully functional on the judging date, and may a recorded demo supplement the live link?

## 5. Practical eligibility preparation

Keep ready, but do not upload publicly:
- Current college enrolment certificate / bonafide certificate or another organiser-accepted undergraduate proof.
- Maharashtra residence proof if claiming eligibility for a finalist invitation.
- Zindi profile details consistent with the official entry form.
- Confirmation that the account/email used in the Google Form matches the Zindi participant.

Do not publish personal identification documents in the GitHub repository.

## 6. Main leaderboard compliance reminders

The organiser-specific discussion has described 10 submissions/day and 140 submissions overall, while the generic full-rules section still contains 300 total and selection-of-two wording. The organiser-specific evaluation post says the latest valid submission is evaluated on the 11–12 Nov private period. Until written confirmation, follow the stricter 10/day and 140-total limits, keep the intended final submission valid/latest, and follow any selection UI if it remains mandatory.

Top 10 private leaderboard participants may be asked to supply code with a short response window (the organiser-specific material describes 48 hours). Prepare reproducible source, config, seeds, data manifest and an end-to-end run before the deadline.
