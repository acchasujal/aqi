## Reference validation limitations

The supplied 24h and 72h leaderboards each show five adjacent 48-hour October 2026 folds, with fold RMSE standard deviations around 0.9–1.8 for the large model set. `run_rolling_cv_evaluation` calls the ensemble optimizer on the assembled OOF prediction matrix and labels, then reports the resulting ensemble on those same OOF rows. The 9.1531 and 9.2545 values therefore include same-OOF weight/calibration fitting, and the candidate zoo, station slopes and shifts are jointly tuned. These are in-sample optimizer results, not independently held-out estimates. Their 24/72 gap, cleaned labels, external features, row support, target scale, and October regime differ from our frozen 84-origin / 12,731-label / raw-target OOF. They are not comparable evidence for our expected RMSE.
