# EXP-012 experiment design

Base: accepted EXP-011 commit `4d7a98b73e4d279e86d510a9e3139d08bddbec81`. Official Train and fixed 2024 archive only. No weather, target-period PM, post-issue information, station remapping, timestamp shift, or hidden targets. PM observations are eligible when their hourly interval ends by the fold cutoff; all calculations use the station's complete prior history with actual timestamps and missing values omitted, never zero-filled.

Candidates, fixed before inspecting EXP-012 scores:

| ID | Formula |
|---|---|
| `legacy_w50` | Existing 50:50 median72 / 2024 seasonal model; production control |
| `median72` | Shared EXP-009 cutoff-anchored 72-hour station median |
| `median24`, `median48` | Median of station PM starts in `[cutoff-hours, cutoff)`, with all-eligible-history median if window empty |
| `trend48` | `max(0, median48 + 0.25 × clip(median48 - median(previous 48h), -20, 20) × exp(-lead_hours/72))`; zero trend if earlier window empty |
| `hybrid_w50_m24` | 50% legacy W50 + 50% median24 |
| `calendar365_w50` | EXP-011 corrected calendar comparator |

All parameters are global, not station-specific. The experiment evaluates 48-hour targets at nominal PM gaps 48/72/96/120h, plus 192h as a competition-style lead diagnostic. Existing accepted EXP-011 row-level OOF data are reused for the four standard gaps; `scripts/validate_calendar_alignment.py` regenerates that cache. The 192h rows are regenerated by the same seasonal OOF function. Candidate rows must match by `(gap, fold, station, target_start)` and raw labels remain unchanged. Recent medians are recomputed from official Train with explicit cutoff and checked against the accepted median72 control. No `available_at` field is used as a publication clock.

Development uses fold cutoffs before `2026-06-25T23:00:00+05:30`; later 2026 folds are a **reused comparison set**, not independent confirmation: EXP-010/011 results from those dates have already been examined. November 2025 is the main November diagnostic, with limited fold count and dates reused across gaps. The official primary metric is full-target RMSE. Ordinary-range RMSE, MAE, station/fold/horizon behavior, extreme SSE contribution and fallback are diagnostics.

Selection rule: name at most one candidate from development full-target and November evidence; compare on the reused late-2026 set without calling it independent confirmation. A submission candidate requires broad, meaningful improvement at the competition-style lead and November-like periods, no major station regression, and no single extreme-driven gain. If that evidence is unavailable or mixed, retain W50 and do not generate a submission.
