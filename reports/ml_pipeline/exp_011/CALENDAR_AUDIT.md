# EXP-011 Calendar alignment audit

**Base:** EXP-010 commit `0270e58c491b87913cae53234edbf3981ffac1a7`. The original `build_seasonal_features` in `src/mumbai_pm25/historical_archive.py` compares raw `day_of_year` from target and 2024 archive rows, using a ±14 integer window and 365-day wrap. The 2024 archive is a leap year; 2025/2026 targets are non-leap. Thus equivalent month/day dates after February 29 are one ordinal apart. The old window for a 2026 November 11 target (DOY 315) is historical DOY 301–329, i.e. October 27–November 24 in 2024; the calendar-aligned window is October 28–November 25. November 12 similarly shifts by one day. This affects historical row selection and the seasonal mean; it does not establish better forecasting accuracy.

The old 365-day wrap is inconsistent with the 366-day archive near New Year. February 29 historical observations can enter old windows. The proposed corrected mode uses a 365-day month/day ordinal, excludes 2024 February 29, and returns a missing seasonal feature for a February 29 target rather than inventing a corresponding date. It preserves inclusive ±14 days (29 calendar dates) and cyclic December/January matching.

The `:00` hourly timestamp is still treated as the historical hour key; the archive's averaging-interval semantics remain unresolved and are not altered. Station substring matching and series pooling remain exactly as in the old mode. All 2024 archive observations precede every target fold in the 2025–2026 Train release, so the fixed historical reference is available at every simulated origin. This does not imply source publication times are verified, but the archive predates the target years.
