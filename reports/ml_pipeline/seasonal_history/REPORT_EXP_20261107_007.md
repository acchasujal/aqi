# EXP-20261107-007: 2024 Seasonal History Climatology and Blend Evaluation

## 1. Executive Summary

This experiment investigates whether historical same-station observations from 2024 supplied in `AdditionalHistory.zip` can provide a useful seasonal prior for Mumbai PM2.5 forecasting, especially under stale PM history conditions (forecast horizons of 48h to 120h).

### Primary Findings
1. **Archive Audit & Acceptance Gate**:
   - `History_Mumbai_2024.csv.gz` contains 216,472 records. Rows from `source == 'us_embassy'` carry half-hourly timestamps (`:30`) and are **rejected**.
   - The remaining 208,043 rows from `source == 'cpcb_caaqm'` have timestamps strictly at minute `:00` and map by name to the four competition stations: Borivali East (15,484 rows), Malad West (21,588 rows), Byculla (8,456 rows), and Shivaji Nagar (5,856 rows).
   - This source **CONDITIONALLY PASSES** as a seasonal/climatological prior (not direct PM replacement). Known caveats: station identity is by name matching only (different operator IDs: IITM/BMC vs CAAQMS); archive PM2.5 values are pre-capped at 1,000 µg/m³; and averaging-interval alignment is unverified.
   - All other archive members (`History_Mumbai_2018_2020.csv.gz`, `Legacy_Observations_2020_2022.csv.gz`, `Other_Mumbai_Stations_2025_2026.csv.gz`) are **REJECTED** from target-station feature generation due to mismatching stations, older regimes, or irregular sampling.

2. **Model Ladder & Benchmark**:
   - Standalone 2024 climatology (`hist24_same_hour`: ±14-day DOY window, same hour-of-day mean) achieves competitive full-target RMSE (43.68 at gap 48h vs 43.98 for median72) and superior clean-range RMSE (21.52 vs 22.66).
   - **`hist24_blend50_median72` (50:50 blend of median72 and 2024 same-hour climatology) is the strongest overall candidate**:
     - **Gap 48h**: Full-target RMSE **43.12** (vs median72 **43.98**; best control `mean72_exclude_gt1000` **43.61**); MAE **12.86** (vs 13.79); Clean RMSE **20.94** (vs 22.66).
     - **Gap 72h**: Full-target RMSE **490.90** (vs median72 **490.97**; best control `mean72_clip300` **490.96**); MAE **18.48** (vs 19.50); Clean RMSE **20.39** (vs 22.23).
     - **Gap 96h**: Full-target RMSE 2348.74 (essentially tied with median72 2348.57, diff 0.17 relative to fold SD >2500); MAE **48.54** (vs 49.64); Clean RMSE **21.00** (vs 22.74).
     - **Gap 120h**: Full-target RMSE 2331.45 (tied with median72 2331.27, diff 0.18); MAE **48.67** (vs 49.90); Clean RMSE **20.29** (vs 21.93).

3. **Consistency across Stations & Horizons**:
   - At Gap 48h, `hist24_blend50_median72` beats `median72` on **all four individual stations** in both RMSE and MAE:
     - Borivali East (11606): RMSE 16.21 vs 17.17; MAE 11.27 vs 12.10
     - Malad West (11611): RMSE 18.69 vs 20.72; MAE 12.81 vs 13.13
     - Byculla (3409510): RMSE 15.22 vs 18.05; MAE 9.74 vs 11.45
     - Shivaji Nagar (3409511): RMSE 81.05 vs 81.62; MAE 17.55 vs 18.44
   - Across all four 12-hour horizon buckets (1–12h, 13–24h, 25–36h, 37–48h), the blend achieves lower RMSE and lower MAE than `median72`.

4. **Recommendation**:
   Adopt `hist24_blend50_median72` as the primary submission strategy. It anchors level to current station observations while borrowing diurnal shape and seasonal climatology from the 2024 archive.

## 3. Matched-Fold Evaluation Protocol

- **Validation strategy**: Leakage-safe chronological rolling validation over 48-hour non-overlapping target windows (7-day step).
- **Folds evaluated**: 85 folds at 48h gap; 87 folds at 72h, 96h, and 120h gaps.
- **Identical rows**: All candidates evaluated on identical eligible (non-NaN) target rows per fold.
- **Labels unchanged**: Validation targets evaluated on raw labels including extreme values. No winsorizing, clipping, or filtering applied to validation targets.
