# EXP-013 competition requirements (checked 2026-11-07 IST)

Official [Zindi data page](https://zindi.world/competitions/google-mumbai-data-challenge/data) confirms the fixed 2,592 `Test.csv`/`SampleSubmission.csv` IDs and order, required `ID,Target` columns with non-negative PM2.5 for every ID, IST timestamps, planned Train updates on November 9 and final November 10 at 09:00 IST, and unchanged test files. The page lists the four CPCB CAAQMS target stations and permits exploring the linked ClimateVerse catalogue. Its generic public-window text says 1–2 November for the 5 November update; the supplied current `DataDictionary.txt` says revised 3–4 November. This conflict is unresolved and is not used as a predictive feature.

The competition-specific [organiser rules and evaluation discussion](https://zindi.world/competitions/google-mumbai-data-challenge/discussions/35720) states **November 10, 2026 at 23:59:59 IST** as the final submission deadline, **10 submissions/day and 140 total**, and the **latest valid submission** evaluated on private November 11–12 hourly PM2.5 by RMSE. It permits core challenge data and relevant ClimateVerse datasets subject to terms, public accessibility and prediction-time availability. The generic Zindi rules embedded below the competition text mention 300 total submissions, selecting two entries, and a generic 20/80 split; these conflict with the competition-specific 140/latest-valid/November-11–12 statements. Apply the stricter competition-specific limit until organiser clarification. Do not infer actual remaining submission quota from this public page; account-specific usage was not inspected.

The local challenge `DataDictionary.txt` defines Train `timestamp_ist` as hourly interval **end** and Test `target_start_ist`/`target_end_ist` as target interval start/end. It says the 5 November local Train has 47,849 rows and the private period is November 11–12 (192 station-hours). Local file audit confirms these counts. The official page lists current file names but does not expose an authenticated downloadable file version/hash in the public HTML; therefore the availability or hash of a newer official Train release cannot be verified here. As of this audit date, the November 9 and 10 scheduled releases are future events. No file was downloaded or replaced.

No hidden targets, future station observations or post-issue forecast products are used. W50 uses the challenge-supplied `AdditionalHistory.zip`; its physical station-series mapping and hourly averaging semantics remain unresolved from EXP-008 and are stated limitations, not silent assumptions.
