# EXP-015 timestamp alignment findings

The competition Train labels use hourly **interval-end** timestamps; `src/mumbai_pm25/data.py` subtracts one hour to align them to Test's interval-start target. This follows the local DataDictionary and [OpenAQ's official date-time documentation](https://docs.openaq.org/using-the-api/dates-datetimes), which describes an hourly record at 03:00 as covering 02:00–02:59. IST offsets are explicit throughout the local files.

The 2024 XKDR archive is different. `HistoryNotes.txt` says its averaging intervals have **not** been fully aligned with current OpenAQ hour-ending targets. All relevant `cpcb_caaqm` timestamps lie on IST hour boundaries, but that proves cadence, not whether the label marks start, end, or an instantaneous reading. The loader currently names these timestamps `hour_start`; this is an assumption. No authoritative XKDR-to-OpenAQ interval rule or simultaneous source overlap was found. The CPCB [CAAQM protocol](https://app.cpcbccr.com/ccr_docs/Protocol_CAAQM.pdf) describes monitoring and reporting cadence, but does not establish the label semantics of this processed XKDR archive.

EXP-015 evaluates historical timestamps at original, −1 hour, and +1 hour on identical historical target rows. This is a **sensitivity analysis**, not an inferred correction. Shifts cross day boundaries as actual timestamps do and retain IST. All archive records precede every simulated EXP-014 issue time, so this operation cannot introduce future PM leakage. November matching and the one-day leap-year mismatch after February 29 remain in the original legacy day-of-year implementation for all three shift scenarios. EXP-011 separately tested the calendar correction and found insufficient evidence to replace W50. No production timestamp or calendar setting is changed.
