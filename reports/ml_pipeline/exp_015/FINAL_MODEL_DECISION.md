# EXP-015 final model decision

**Retain `hist24_blend50_median72` (W50).** The Mindspace-Malad geographic mismatch is confirmed, but its isolated, explicitly named `exclude_mindspace` scenario worsens public and private full-target RMSE, ordinary-range RMSE, Malad station RMSE, and November RMSE on the fixed EXP-014 rows. It changes 648 current Test predictions, so treating it as a harmless cleanup would be incorrect. Same-coordinate Borivali and Malad archive series remain unverified against the OpenAQ sensors. A future source crosswalk or fresh confirmation fold may warrant revisiting the mapping; this experiment does not silently change production behavior.

The −1-hour archive shift has slightly lower historical RMSE (public 1913.792 versus 1913.800; private 1918.157 versus 1918.166; November public 35.154 versus 35.369 and private 44.039 versus 44.226), and improves 7/11 folds in each regime. However, the archive's hour-label semantics are unresolved, all EXP-014 station-hours were inspected in EXP-012, and five repeated extreme labels dominate full-target RMSE. A shift cannot be justified from an exploratory score difference. +1 hour is mixed and likewise unsupported. Neither shift is adopted.

Production W50 was regenerated on the unchanged local official snapshot with the original cutoff and timestamp. Its 2,592-row CSV exactly reproduces SHA-256 `55e2fc8c7cad94db22f1358a8715216b0c7fbbb7a50c728c4413890f6a8716bc`, with exact SampleSubmission IDs/order and finite nonnegative values. The regression artifact is ignored under `data/processed/`; no cosmetic new submission is created. EXP-014's robust supervised candidate improves public ordinary-range errors but regresses on the private and November target regimes, so it remains unselected.

This is an evidence-limited **no-challenger** decision, not evidence that W50 is optimal. The highest-value next action is a final official Train refresh and a predeclared comparison on genuinely new, chronologically eligible labels. Separately, request an organizer/provider crosswalk for XKDR `site_*` series to OpenAQ `sensor_id` and an explicit XKDR hourly interval convention. Keep all extreme labels intact unless the organizer publishes a correction or independent quality evidence.
