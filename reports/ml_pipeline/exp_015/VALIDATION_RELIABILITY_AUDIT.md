# EXP-015 validation reliability audit

EXP-009 through EXP-014 progressively improved simulation fidelity but did not produce an untouched confirmation set. EXP-009/010's nominal 48/72/96/120-hour gaps do not reproduce the current private 193–240-hour observation-to-target leads. EXP-011/012 added calendar, hybrid, and long-gap diagnostics. EXP-013 fixed the private target shape: 85 weekly folds, 13,244 matched station-hours per model, actual lead 193–240 hours after filtering. Only one fold had all 192 possible station-hours; historical label missingness and station-specific outages make coverage incomplete. EXP-013 compared its keys with EXP-012 and found **100% of matched station-target rows previously scored**. Its fold calendar is thus exploratory.

EXP-014 selected nine fixed development target days and two known extreme stress days, each evaluated at public and private issue cutoffs. Its 2024 seasonal archive predates every simulated issue; PM features require observation interval end no later than cutoff, and supervised training examples require target interval end no later than validation PM cutoff. These gates were tested in EXP-014. `available_at` in Train is **not verified first-publication time**; the simulated PM cutoff is a conservative availability proxy rather than a verified real release log. EXP-014's own leakage audit says prior experiments inspected overlapping periods. Its severe extreme stress dates were known before model comparison. EXP-015 uses its cached W50 rows as a reproducible diagnostic set, not a new independent holdout.

`FOLD_LINEAGE.json` quantifies unique EXP-014 station-hours, repetitions under different origins, and overlap with local EXP-012/013 row caches. The same underlying label can be scored under a public and private historical origin, creating correlated squared errors. EXP-015's bounded interventions are compared on identical keyed rows and unmodified labels, but their results remain exploratory. The 2026 November private window has no accessible labels. No current historical re-partition can erase the fact that these periods have already influenced team decisions. Genuinely new organizer-released Train labels after this snapshot are the next possible confirmation source.

The keyed audit found 3,430 EXP-014 W50 scored rows but only 1,719 unique station-hours; 1,711 are counted twice under the different public/private origins. **All 3,430** station-hours had appeared in EXP-012's 40,546-row unique-key cache. EXP-013 had previously scored 3,422 of these 3,430 rows (1,711 unique keys). EXP-015 therefore has zero genuinely unseen scored station-hours in this OOF selection. `TARGETED_FOLD_RESULTS.csv` records the 11 target-day folds per regime and their candidate differences; these are descriptive repeats, not independent confirmation.

Fold lineage sources: `data/processed/exp012_row_predictions.csv.gz`, `exp013_row_predictions.csv.gz`, and `exp014_oof.csv.gz` are **ignored local caches** copied unchanged from the respective experiment worktrees. Regenerate with their original validation scripts to audit the lineage. The EXP-015 script reads these caches, asserts that the archive predates origins, and computes all comparisons on fixed EXP-014 rows. The reconstructed public-shaped OOF begins at lead 2 rather than lead 1 in some folds due to historical cutoff/grid details; private leads similarly begin at 194 in some folds. Therefore the historical template approximates rather than exactly mirrors the live challenge lead histogram.
