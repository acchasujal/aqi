# EXP-014 model selection

**Select no supervised challenger. Retain W50.** CatBoost Huber is the best supervised candidate in the bounded search, but it fails the private-horizon and November transfer criteria. On all identical public-shaped rows, its full-target RMSE is 1913.6724 versus 1913.7996 for W50, a 0.1273 gain dominated by five extreme labels and far below fold variability. On private-shaped rows it scores 1918.1244 versus 1918.1655, an even smaller 0.0411 gain. The ordinary-range diagnostic improves public RMSE by 3.87 yet **worsens private RMSE by 6.75**. In November, it improves public RMSE by 6.05 but worsens private RMSE by 18.81. It wins 9/11 public folds and 7/11 private folds, but the private losses include the larger November regressions. Its Malad and Byculla private station RMSEs are worse than W50.

The squared-error LightGBM, CatBoost, HistGradientBoosting and residual LightGBM candidates all have substantially worse full RMSE and several produce implausibly large non-extreme predictions after training on official extreme labels. No model was tuned on the public leaderboard. No parameter search was run after seeing these results. The fixed stress periods were identified in earlier audits and cannot serve as independent confirmation. All 22 validation folds are exploratory because the periods overlap earlier research and because historical release timing is proxied by explicit cutoffs rather than verified first-publication times.

In-memory current-snapshot CatBoost Huber inference was finite and non-negative on the 384 public/private target rows, changed all 384 W50 predictions and ranged **25.093–58.017** µg/m³. Its pickled model predicted identically after restoration and its W50 feature matched production exactly. This demonstrates technical viability, not enough accuracy evidence for a submission. No challenger CSV or model binary was saved. The existing W50 submission remains the only prepared artifact and production default.
