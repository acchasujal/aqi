# AirWindow Mumbai usability test protocol

**Purpose:** Find comprehension and workflow failures in the event-planning comparison. This is a protocol only; no participants or results are claimed.

## Participants and consent

Recruit three to five adults who organise school events, sports activities or comparable outdoor events. Do not recruit minors. Participation is voluntary. Explain that the prototype provides a station-level PM2.5 planning comparison, not a safety or health recommendation. Ask permission before taking notes or recording a screen. Do not collect names, school names, precise venues, health information, contact details or identifiable schedules. Use participant codes P1-P5; store notes locally with access limited to the project owner and delete raw notes after the findings summary is agreed.

Read this consent prompt: “I’m evaluating the AirWindow interface, not you. You can stop at any time. I will record task outcomes and usability issues without your name or school. The forecast is a frozen prototype, and monitoring stations may not represent your venue. May I take anonymised notes while you try it?” Record yes/no; do not proceed without yes.

## Task

Ask the participant to imagine a two-hour school sports activity already booked for 10:00-12:00, with a feasible move between 08:00 and 18:00. Using the public prototype, compare the original and alternatives for one station/date. Ask them to decide what they would tell a colleague, including whether the evidence supports changing the booking. Do not coach unless they are blocked; mark any help given.

## Five task-completion questions

1. Did the participant find the station, date, duration and original booking controls without help?
2. Did they produce a comparison with the requested activity duration and a complete feasible window?
3. Could they explain the forecast difference and identify which time was the original booking?
4. Could they locate the forecast issue time, PM history cutoff and validity end?
5. Could they distinguish retrospective aggregate evidence from the selected case's later observation, and explain why AirWindow may abstain?

## Observation sheet

For each task, record `success / partial / not completed`, elapsed time only if the participant agrees, help prompts, exact control or wording that caused confusion, and whether the participant recovered unaided. Record bugs separately from misunderstandings. Note screen size and browser, but no device/account identifiers. Never interpret one participant's preference as general demand.
